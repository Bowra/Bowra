## See and copy the version

- Open the account menu in the lower-left corner (on a phone, tap the menu button at the top left; your account is at the bottom of the panel). Its last line is the current version, for example “{applicationName} 1.4.8”.
- Click that line, or move to it with the keyboard and press Enter, to copy the full version details. A “Version details copied” message confirms it.
- The **Copy version details** button at the top of this page copies the same text.

When you report a problem to your team administrator or to support, include these version details so they can tell which version you are seeing.

## When the web app and server versions differ

The version details have a “Web app” line and a “Server” line. They are normally the same. If they differ, your browser is still running an older copy of the page: refresh the page to load the latest version. Save anything you are editing first.

## Release notes

Open **Release notes** from the account menu, between **Help Center** and **Language**, to read released versions on a separate page. The newest versions appear by default, with as many complete version groups as the navigation can fit, up to ten and at least one. The default adjusts when you resize the window; versions you load manually stay available. You can also use the link at the top of this About page.

**Release notes** lists the changes that users and administrators will notice, by released version, newest first.

Click **Load more** at the bottom of the version menu to show three older versions at a time, down to v1.5.0, without moving your reading position. The bottom of the menu shows how many versions are displayed and confirms when all history is visible. Publication dates appear beside each version and in its notes.

Use the search box on the left to find a version number or words across all published notes, including versions you have not loaded. Select a version or its sections in the menu to jump to it. Matching sections show a short preview. On a phone, search and navigation appear above the notes. Search shows only matching versions and highlights matching words in their notes. Clear the search to restore your previous version list and reading position. The menu highlights the section you are reading as you scroll; the current version stays available when you resize the window.

## Edition and language

The Community edition is named **Bowra** and the Business edition is named **Bowhead**. Both use the same whale symbol; a Business team may display its configured organization brand. Community first-owner setup needs no commercial license, renewal, or built-in user allowance. Administrator-set usage budgets and normal permissions still apply.

Your interface and answer language preferences are independent of the edition. Without a saved interface preference, the browser or system language is used, with English as the fallback.
