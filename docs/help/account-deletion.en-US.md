# Delete your account

Open **Settings → Account → Delete account…**. Enter the account email and current password. If you sign in without a password, sign out and sign in again, then confirm within five minutes.

Deletion permanently retires your account and releases its email. You cannot undo it. A new account with the same email starts independently and receives none of your old conversations, files, permissions, connections or settings. Normal registration or invitation requirements still apply.

Your personal history and files are isolated for 30 days from the deletion request, then automatically scheduled for permanent cleanup. Storage failures can delay completion; your administrator can investigate the deletion operation. Credentials are revoked immediately, and temporary execution material can expire earlier under its existing policy.

Team-owned knowledge and shared resources remain with the team. A personal file shared with someone else is still personal and is removed with your data. Transfer team ownership, last-administrator responsibilities for Teams and Spaces and active shared Agents before deleting your account. Export or download anything you need first.

If your password is incorrect, re-enter or reset it. If a team or Agent requires transfer, complete that action in its team settings and return here. If cleanup is unavailable or the result is uncertain, contact your administrator before submitting again. Files already downloaded or copied independently by other people cannot be recalled.
