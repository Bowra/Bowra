# Explore Agent charts

Open **Agents → Top Charts** in the sidebar. **Marketplace**, **My Agents** and **Top Charts** are separate sidebar pages. Clicking **Agents** opens **My agents → Hired by me**.

- **Top** shows the Agents used by the most members of your current team over the past 28 days.
- **Trending** shows growth in active members over the past 7 days compared with the previous 7 days.
- **Recently Updated** shows saved changes to team Agent listings. It does not mean newly published or most popular.

Use the **Category: All** button to narrow the list, and choose **How the charts work** to expand or collapse how the charts are counted. Choose **Details** to inspect an Agent and the actions available to you. **Back to Top Charts** returns to the same chart and category.

Charts need supported team statistics and enough real use to meet privacy requirements. If a chart is unavailable, has too little data, or has expired, no ranking or usage counts are shown. You can still open recent updates or browse Agents; use **Retry** when a request fails.

Charts update automatically from successful member use when statistics are available. Top requires a complete 28-day window; Trending requires two complete 7-day windows. During initial collection, the page explains that the full window is still being collected. Every displayed usage period and current adoption count must each include at least five eligible members. Counts are team aggregates. Popularity is not a quality score and does not grant access to an Agent or its data.

When the earliest complete-window date is known, the collecting state shows it in your browser's time zone. This is the earliest possible window completion, not a promise that rankings will appear that day: eligible member counts must still meet privacy requirements.
