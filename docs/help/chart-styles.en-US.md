# Choose or publish a chart style

## Choose a personal style

Open **Settings → Appearance** and find the chart section. Use the Team default or select one of the styles your Team allows. Adjust high contrast and color-vision friendly patterns separately. Check the preview before working with charts whose series are hard to distinguish.

## Publish for the Team

Team owners and administrators open the chart style library under **Team settings → Brand & Appearance** in Business, or **Chart Styles** in Community. Create a style from an available template, name it, and open its editor. Adjust the chart elements, use the preview to check how the changes read, then save the draft and publish the reviewed version.

On the overview, choose which styles members can use and which is the Team default. Save the policy. If a style is unavailable to a member, check both whether it has a published version and whether the policy offers it. Use a style’s revision history to restore an earlier version when needed. [Saving and confirming changes](settings-save.en-US.md)
