# Conversation links and browser navigation

The address bar always matches what you are looking at: when you open a conversation, the address includes that conversation; when you go back to the home page to start new work, the address returns to the home page. Copying the address to a colleague who has access, refreshing the page, or following a notification all open the same conversation.

For an address under a path such as `/bowhead/`, keep the slash before `?session=…`. If an older saved link sends you to an unexpected server port, reopen the complete address your team gave you and ask the administrator to check its entry redirect. Do not add an internal server port yourself.

When you use the browser's back and forward buttons, the page follows the address: going back to a conversation opens it, and going forward to the home page returns you to **New Work**, just like choosing **New Work** on the left. Deleting the open conversation from the home page, or selecting the brand logo in the top-left corner, also returns you to **New Work**. Anything you typed but did not send in the home composer is kept.

If the current report has unsaved changes, you are asked before leaving. **Save and switch** saves the changes and goes to the home page. **Stay on this report** keeps you in the conversation, and the address returns to it; your changes are not affected. In that case the browser history keeps two entries for this conversation next to each other, so the next time you press back the page appears unchanged; press back once more to reach the earlier page.
