This Help Center helps you complete work and understand how your team collaborates safely. Start with the outcome you need; you do not need to read every guide first.

## How this guide is organized

The guide has three levels: this home page, topic areas, and task guides. Article sections explain decisions, steps, confirmation, and troubleshooting without adding more sidebar levels.

- **Getting started** explains how to start work, provide context, and review results.
- **Interface and navigation** covers menus, list actions, mobile dialogs and undo, conversation links, and language settings.
- **Team and access** explains how teams, spaces, and members set the collaboration boundary.
- **Agents and Skills** explains how reusable methods are published, hired, configured, and maintained.
- **Security and data** explains how data is accessed under each person's identity and how AI-generated code runs in an offline sandbox.
- **Reference** is for quickly checking a role, action, or permission problem.

## Remember three boundaries

1. **Teams and spaces** decide where people collaborate. They do not automatically grant access to external business data.
2. **Agents, Capabilities, Skills, and tools** decide how work is done. Being able to use an Agent does not grant permission to edit or publish it.
3. **Your data identity** decides what a query can see. Sharing an Agent, report, or space never transfers the author's data permissions.

If a page says you do not have access, confirm the account, team, and space first. Record the message and resource name, then follow [Troubleshoot access](permissions/troubleshoot-access.en-US.md). If credits are exhausted, see [View and allocate credits](credits.en-US.md).
