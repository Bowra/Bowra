# Language settings

Bowra's interface and AI answers are available in Simplified Chinese, English, German, and French. You can choose the interface language and the AI answer language separately.

On your first visit, Bowra follows your browser language, which usually reflects your system language. Unsupported languages use English. New accounts keep automatic language selection; until you choose an answer language, AI answers use the language you ask in. Your existing explicit choices are preserved.

## Change the interface language

The interface language changes only the text on buttons, menus, and pages, never the language of AI answers. There are two places to change it, with the same options:

- **Account menu**: select your avatar in the bottom-left corner (on a phone, at the bottom of the sidebar) and find the **Language** row, which shows the current language. On a computer, hover over **Language**, or move to it with the arrow keys and press →, to open the list; press ← or Esc to go back. On a phone, tap **Language**; the panel switches to the list. Tap “‹ Account” in the top-left corner to return.
- **Personal Settings → Account → General preferences → Interface language**: select the button to open the same list.

The first option is **Automatic**; its second line names the language it uses right now (your browser's language, or English when that language is not supported). Below it are “中文”, “English”, “Deutsch”, and “Français”, with a check before your current choice. After choosing a specific language you can choose **Automatic** again to follow your browser.

After you choose, the button shows “Switching to …”, and once the choice is saved (within about 2 seconds) the page reloads once in the new language. Choosing the option that is already checked does nothing. When you switch in Personal Settings with unsaved changes at the bottom of the page, you are asked first whether to discard them.

On the sign-in, registration, activation, and invitation pages, the language button below the card lists the same four languages (without **Automatic**); the page reloads once after you choose, and an email address you already typed is kept.

The last item in the account menu's **Language** list, **Answer language settings**, opens **Answer language** in Personal Settings and shows your current answer language.

Dates and numbers are displayed using the conventions of the selected interface language.

Built-in Tool, Capability, Skill, and Agent names use English by default and show available translations in your interface language. Names you or your administrator have chosen stay unchanged when you switch languages.

Bowra's notice cards in Feishu, Slack, and Teams, and scheduled task result emails, use the interface language you have chosen. If you have not chosen one, they follow the language you write in (for scheduled tasks, the language of the task's question), and English when that cannot be told.

## Choose the AI answer language

If you have not chosen an answer language and your team does not require one, Bowra answers in the language you ask in: ask in Chinese and the answer is in Chinese, ask in English and it is in English. This applies to web chat, Feishu, Slack, and Teams; scheduled tasks answer in the language of the task's question. A reply such as “1”, “ok”, numbers only, or an emoji keeps the language of the previous answer in that conversation. A Chinese question that mentions English product names or metrics is still answered in Chinese. If the first message of a conversation has no words, your team's default answer language is used, or the interface language if the team has none.

Choose it in **Personal Settings → Account → General preferences → Answer language**: **Match My Question** or a specific language. Your choice is saved at once, without reloading the page. With **Match my question**, a message that shows no language keeps the conversation's previous answer language, then falls back to your team's default answer language. If your team requires an answer language, the button is unavailable and the line below it names the language your team set. German appears in the list only when German AI answers are enabled for your deployment.

You can also tell Bowra in a chat which language you want future answers in, for example “Please answer in German from now on.” Bowra saves this as the same answer language preference. You can also ask it to always follow the language of each question. If your team has set a default answer language and you have not chosen one, it is used only when your message gives no language. If your team requires an answer language, the team setting applies. If a question says “please answer in English”, that answer uses the language you named.

This setting affects AI answers, report text, and chart titles. Names of organizations, products, and people in your data stay in their original language. Number and date formats in charts inside the app follow the interface language.

German AI answers may not be enabled yet while they are being rolled out. In that case the interface can still be shown in German, but AI answers are written in English. Contact your administrator if you need answers and reports in German.

AI answers in French are available now. Report labels and source notes also support French.

## Space language defaults

Space administrators can open **Space settings → General** to edit the Space name and description under **Basic information**, and manage data language, the default interface language, and the first day of the week in the language settings below. Name and description changes use the save bar; language settings save immediately. If **Open in Canvas** is available in the page header, save or discard pending changes before opening it.
