# Use menus

Drop-down menus across Bowra (the account menu next to your avatar, the model and plan mode in the composer, the space switcher, export, the “⋯” at the end of a row, and so on) work in a consistent way.

## Read release notes

Open **Release notes** in the account menu, between **Help Center** and **Language**. The independent page lists the latest three published versions, newest first. Help Center → About also links to that page.

## On a phone

- Menus with more than 6 items, with descriptions, or with a further level of options open as a panel from the bottom of the screen, for example the account menu, plan mode, export, and the space switcher when there are many spaces or spaces with descriptions. The panel has a short bar at the top: drag it down, or tap the gray area outside the panel, to close it. While the panel is open, the page behind it can't be used and doesn't scroll.
- Menus with only a few simple actions (such as a conversation's “⋯”) still open next to the button, with rows large enough to tap easily.
- When there are many models to choose from, selecting the model button below the composer opens a **Model** panel from the bottom, where you can see full names and switch models.

## Find settings and admin pages

Open **Settings** from your account menu for Personal, Team and current Space settings. Open **Administration Console** for the operations and governance pages available to your role. Each area has its own sidebar directory.

- The main sidebar keeps New Work, My Reports and Library first, followed by **Agents**, then **Capability Center**, including on capability resource pages. Agents opens the market; use Manage My Agents for its existing management views.
- Capability Center opens its overview and expands **Skills and Workflows**, **Tools**, **Team Plugins**, **My Connections** and **Scheduled Tasks**. Availability follows your account's permissions and enabled features.
- Agent Tabs and Skill filters stay inside their pages. Agent details and management keep Agents selected; other catalogs and configuration keep their corresponding resource selected. Use a resource's management action when available, then its return link to go back. Management opened from Team Management returns to that overview view; management opened from the Skills page's Mine filter returns to Mine. Opening management links in another browser tab also retains the supported return context.
- Search the Settings or Administration directory by name, or search within a resource catalog. Counts show pending decisions or active scheduled tasks. In Settings and Administration, **Back to Work** returns to your previous work page.
- On narrow screens, use the top-left menu button to open the same navigation. In the compact rail, the Capability Center icon opens a menu with its overview and resource pages; Settings and Administration retain their group menus.

## Change the interface language

The language setting is in the account menu. See [Language settings](language.en-US.md) for the steps.

## Red actions ending in “…”

Red menu actions whose names end in “…” (for example **Delete report…**, **Clear report…**, or **Remove from Team…**) delete content or remove access. Selecting one first opens a confirmation that explains what will happen; choosing **Cancel** or pressing Esc changes nothing.

Deletions that can be undone are the exception: **Delete Session**, moving a memory to the trash, and **Delete** on the Files page don't ask for confirmation and don't end in “…”. Afterwards, **Undo** appears at the top of the page for at least 8 seconds; select it to restore the item (see [Mobile dialogs and undo](mobile-dialogs-undo.en-US.md)). A file that a report links to still shows a warning first that downloads in the report will stop working.

## Where to delete a conversation

Deleting a conversation is in the “⋯” on that conversation's row in the sidebar, and in the “⋯” to the right of the open conversation's title (on a phone, at the right of the top bar). Both menus have the same items in the same order:

- On a computer: hover over a conversation in the sidebar, and “⋯” appears at the end of the row; select “⋯” and choose **Delete Session**. **Pin** and **Archive Session** are also in the “⋯” menu, and the row shows only “⋯”. You can also press Tab to reach “⋯”, press Enter or Down Arrow to open the menu, use the arrow keys to choose, and press Esc to close it. When the sidebar is collapsed, open the list from the **Recent Sessions** icon and do the same.
- Conversations you haven't viewed yet have a bold title; screen readers say “Unread” after the title.
- On a phone: open the sidebar; the “⋯” on each conversation row is always shown.
- A running conversation can't be deleted. Wait until it finishes or stop it first.

Deleting a conversation doesn't ask for confirmation. Afterwards, **Undo** appears at the top of the page for at least 8 seconds.

## Rename a conversation

1. Open the “⋯” on the conversation's row in the sidebar (on a phone, open the sidebar first), or the “⋯” to the right of the open conversation's title (on a phone, at the right of the top bar).
2. Choose **Rename…**. The current name is selected, so you can type over it.
3. Enter the new name (up to 100 characters) and press Enter or select **Save**. Press Esc or select **Cancel** to keep the old name.

The new name appears in the sidebar and the title bar right away. A name you choose is kept: the automatic title made after the first answer never replaces it. You can rename only your own conversations. If saving fails, the old name comes back and a message says why.

## Gray actions you can't select

A gray menu action can't be used right now, and the reason is shown under it, for example “This work is still running. Finish or stop it before archiving.” or “Only the uploader or a space admin can delete this file.” Once you deal with the reason, the action becomes available. When you browse a menu with the keyboard, the arrow keys also stop on these items so screen readers can read the reason.

## Use the keyboard

- After opening a menu, use ↑ and ↓ to choose, Enter to run the action, and Esc to close. When the menu closes, focus returns to the button that opened it.
- When you type “@” in the composer to reference content, or open the space switcher, a hint listing these keys appears at the bottom of the list when you use a mouse on a computer.
