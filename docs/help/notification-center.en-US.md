# Notifications

These notification options are available when enabled by your deployment administrator.

The bell at the top of the page collects what happened while you were working elsewhere: approvals waiting for you, scheduled tasks that failed or need an answer, access requests, shared content, and results of things you started.

## What the bell shows

- A **number** on the bell counts the items that need you to do something. A **dot** means there are only unread updates.
- **Action** lists what needs you. An item stays here until the thing itself is handled (the approval is decided, the task is answered or resumed, the request is closed), even after you read it.
- **Updates** lists everything else, such as decisions on your requests and shared content. **Mark all read** clears their unread state.
- **All** shows both.

## Act on an item

1. Select the bell.
2. Select an item to open the page where you handle it. Items that need you also show buttons, such as **Review** or **Answer**; the first one is the usual next step.
3. To remove an item from your own list, select **Archive** on its row. Archiving does not handle it for anyone else.

## Items from your other teams

If you belong to more than one team, each item shows the name of the team it came from. Opening an item from another team switches you to that team first, then opens the item. If that team uses its own web address, Bowhead opens that address instead; open the item again from the bell there.

## Pop-ups and browser notifications

While Bowhead is open in a tab, urgent items that need you, such as an approval waiting for you or a scheduled task waiting for your answer, appear as a pop-up with **Open**. The first time something is announced, Bowhead asks once whether to notify you while the tab is in the background; choose **Turn on** and allow it in the browser.

- Browser notifications appear only while the Bowhead tab is open in the background, and only on a secure (HTTPS) address.
- Items that arrived while Bowhead was closed are not announced again; they are counted on the bell.
- With several Bowhead tabs open, each item is announced once.
- To stop browser notifications, turn them off for this site in your browser's site settings.

## Notes

- Reading an item does not handle it. Something that needs you leaves **Action** only when it is handled or no longer applies.
- You only see items addressed to you. Opening one still checks your current permissions on the page it opens.
