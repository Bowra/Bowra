# Reminder emails

These notification options are available when enabled by your deployment administrator.

Bowhead can email you about things that are waiting on you, so they don't sit unnoticed while you are away.

## When you get a reminder

- **An approval waiting for you.** If you approve tool actions for your team and a request is still open and unread 30 minutes after it arrived, you get one email.
- **A scheduled task waiting for your answer.** If your task paused to ask you something and you haven't read the notification after 30 minutes, you get one email.

You get one reminder per item. If you read it, archive it or it is handled before then, no email is sent.

## Turn reminder emails off or on

1. Open **Settings → Profile**.
2. Under **Preferences**, set **Reminder emails** to **Off** (or back to **On**).

The change saves at once and also stops reminders that are already due. It does not affect emails a scheduled task's owner set up, such as result emails and urgent task alerts.

## See whether a task's email went out

Open **Team settings → Scheduled tasks** and select **Run history** for the task. Under each run you can see whether its result email or alert email was sent. When one was not sent, the line says why and what to do, with a reference you can give your administrator.

## Notes

- Emails go only to your own account address, and only while you are an active member of the team the item belongs to.
- Your team's mail service must be set up; otherwise reminders are not sent and the task's run history says so.
