# Space roles and collaboration access

A space is the collaboration boundary inside a team. Space roles decide who can work there and manage its membership; they do not transfer personal data permissions or ownership of every shared resource.

## Space roles

| Role                | What it is for                                                                                      |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Space administrator | Manages the space, its membership, and supported space settings.                                    |
| Collaborator        | Works with resources shared in the space, subject to each resource's audience and data permissions. |
| Viewer              | Reads content shared for viewing, without ordinary editing or management actions.                   |

## Join and manage a space

A space administrator adds a member and chooses the role. The person should then refresh and confirm the selected space is visible. Change a role when responsibilities change; remove a person when they no longer need the collaboration area. Team administrators do not automatically become space administrators, and a space administrator does not gain team-wide configuration rights.

## What members can see

Membership makes the space available. Each report, attachment, Agent, Skill, and data source still applies its own sharing and authorization rules. A person can read a shared report while lacking access to its original files or the ability to rerun its query.

When a space is archived or a person is removed, future access through that space ends. Preserve or transfer any material that must remain available through a supported sharing path before removing access.
