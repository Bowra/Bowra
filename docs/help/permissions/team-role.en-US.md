# Assign a team administrator

Use this guide to delegate team governance. Team administrators can manage supported team-wide settings and membership, so choose people who need this responsibility and use a space role for narrower work.

## Before you start

You must be the team owner or have the product permission to change team roles. Confirm the person's account and the specific responsibilities they will take on. A team role does not grant personal MCP access, private resource ownership, or another person's data identity.

## Change the role

1. Open **Team settings → Members**.
2. Find the member and choose **Change team role**.
3. Select **Team administrator** and save.
4. Reopen the member row and confirm the displayed role.

The new administrator can manage the team features available to that role. They may still need to be added as a space administrator for a particular space, and should use their own account and data authorization for every task.

To return the person to the ordinary member role, repeat the same steps and select **Member**. Do this when the governance responsibility ends, then review any spaces where they remain an administrator.
