# Team roles and permissions

Team roles govern team-wide administration. They do not automatically grant access to every space, Agent draft, attachment, or external business-data source.

## The four team roles

| Role                | Main responsibility                                                                                    |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Team owner          | Owns the team and can manage team-wide governance, members, settings, and ownership transfer.          |
| Team administrator  | Manages the team settings and members permitted by the product.                                        |
| Audit administrator | Has read-only team visibility; the enterprise audit workbench and export require the Business edition. |
| Member              | Uses the resources and spaces shared with them.                                                        |

## What team roles can manage

Team owners and administrators manage team members, supported team services, approval flows, and team-level configuration. They still need a space role to manage a particular space, and they cannot use another member's personal MCP connection or data identity. Agent authors retain control of the content and versions they created unless a product workflow explicitly assigns a manager.

## Check or change a role

Open **Team settings → Members**, find the person, and review their current role. A team owner or permitted administrator can select the new role and save it. Reopen the member record to confirm the update. Use the least powerful role that lets the person perform their job.

If you only manage one collaboration area, ask to become a space administrator instead of a team administrator. For a denied action, first identify whether it is team governance, space administration, resource editing, or data access; each has a different owner.
