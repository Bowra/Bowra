# Manage your account and personal settings

Open **Settings** from the account menu. The personal pages apply to your account.

## Account

Edit your display name and save. The email row shows whether the address is verified and offers to resend verification when needed. Set the interface and AI answer languages independently. Under **Agents**, choose whether Coach should check a draft automatically after editing or ask first. Your current tier and credit balance are in **Billing**. [Language settings](language.en-US.md) · [Reminder emails](notification-emails.en-US.md)

## Security

Open **Security → Change password** and enter your current and new passwords. A successful change signs out your other devices. Use the reset link if you forgot the password. If your account signs in only through an external provider, manage its password there.

Under **Active sessions**, review the latest recorded activity for each listed device. Logins with the same browser details and network address share a row. Use the row menu to revoke the listed sessions. On the current device, the menu can revoke its other sessions while keeping this session open. **Log out everywhere** signs out the current session too.

## Appearance

Choose system, light or dark mode, then adjust color, background, font, text size, density, corners or motion. The page shows the effective appearance and whether a setting comes from you, the team or the product. Clear a personal choice to inherit the default. **Clear personal appearance** clears the personal theme and reading preferences. Chart style and chart accessibility options have their own controls. [Choose or publish a chart style](chart-styles.en-US.md)

## Export

Open the report itself and choose **Export**, under **More** on a narrow screen. Select the format offered for that report. The personal Export page explains the options. To fill a specific uploaded PowerPoint, send it as a template in Chat. [Fill or update a PowerPoint template](powerpoint-templates.en-US.md)
