# Space instructions

Space instructions are a short text that every conversation in a space follows. Use them for what everyone in the space would otherwise have to repeat: who the answers are for, which comparisons to show by default, the preferred tone or length, or background about the space's work. They apply to every member of the space and to every Agent that works in it, including built-in and team Agents.

## Write or change the instructions

Space administrators and team administrators can change the instructions. Team administrators do not need to join the space.

1. As a space administrator, open the space and choose **Space settings → Instructions**. As a team administrator, open **Team settings → Spaces**, open the **More actions** menu of the space and choose **Instructions**.
2. Write the instructions in plain language. For example: "Answers are for regional sales managers. Compare with the same month last year by default and keep summaries short."
3. Select **Save**.

The instructions apply from the next message sent in the space. A reply that is already being written keeps the version it started with. To stop using instructions, clear the text and save.

In the Team settings dialog, Save sits at the bottom of the dialog and the dialog stays open after saving. If you close it with unsaved changes, the bottom bar asks whether to keep editing or discard them.

## Ask in chat to change them

You can also ask in a conversation in the space, for example: "Add to this space's instructions: new products are those launched within the last 24 months." The assistant reads the current instructions and shows a suggestion card with the lines it would add or remove. Nothing is saved until you select **Apply** on the card, and the saved text becomes a new version. Read every line before applying: the assistant rewrites the whole text, so check that lines you did not ask about are unchanged.

- Only space administrators and team administrators get a suggestion card. Other members can ask the assistant what the instructions say.
- If someone saves the instructions after the suggestion was made, applying is blocked so their change is not overwritten. Ask again in chat for a suggestion based on the current text.
- An applied suggestion has no Undo on the card. To undo it, restore the previous version under **Version history**.

## See the instructions as a member

Every member can read the instructions of the space they are in. Select the **About this Space** button (the ⓘ icon next to the space switcher at the top). It shows the instructions, when they were last saved and which space administrators to ask for a change. You can also open **Instructions** under the space group in **Settings** to read the full text.

## Add an adopted suggestion

Members can suggest a definition or wording in a conversation, and it reaches the team suggestion inbox. When a team administrator adopts such a suggestion, the inbox lists each space the suggestion came from, with how many conversations raised it there.

1. Select **Add to instructions** on a space's row.
2. Review the comparison: it shows the end of the current instructions and the lines the suggestion adds. The text comes from member feedback, so read it line by line. If the instructions already have a similar line, a note points to it so you can merge them.
3. Select **I have reviewed the prefilled content line by line**, then **Apply to editor**. Edit the text if needed and select **Save**. Nothing is saved before that.

After saving, the row shows the version the wording was added in, so it is not added twice. To come back later, switch the inbox to **Processed**: adopted Business-definition suggestions list their source spaces again with what was already added. Repeat for the other spaces as needed. If the conversations behind the suggestion are no longer in an active space, copy the text and choose the space yourself from **Team settings → Spaces**.

## Go back to an earlier version

Every save is kept as a version. Under **Version history**, select a version to see what it changed compared with the version before it, or select **View full text**. Select **Restore this version** to load it into the editor. A note above the editor says which version you are restoring; select **Undo** to get your previous text back. Select **Save as version N** to keep it. Restoring creates a new version; the history stays intact.

## Good to know

- A member's own request in a conversation, and their personal memory, take precedence over the space instructions for that member.
- The limit is 6,000 characters. A longer text cannot be saved; the page shows the current length.
- If another administrator saves while you are editing, your save is stopped so their change is not overwritten without notice. Your text stays in the editor, and the version history opens their version with the lines they changed. Review it, then select **Save my text anyway** if your text should replace it, or **Discard my changes**.
- Instructions of an archived space cannot be changed until the space is restored.
- Evaluations of Agent versions do not use space instructions, so evaluation results show how the Agent behaves on its own.
- Members who are not space administrators cannot change the instructions. If something should be different, ask a space administrator, or suggest it in a conversation so it reaches the suggestion inbox.
