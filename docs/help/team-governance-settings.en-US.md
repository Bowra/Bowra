# Review suggestions and manage reference retention

## Feedback Inbox

Open **Team settings → Feedback Inbox**. Use **Waiting** to find suggestions you can act on and **Processed** to review decisions. Open the details for the suggestion, evidence and any source conversations you can access.

If **Adopt** is available, review the content in the destination editor and save it there. Adopting a suggestion prepares a change for review. An Agent change goes through the Agent owner’s editor and release steps. Use **Reject**, **Reroute**, or **Withdraw** when offered for your role and the suggestion’s state. [Publish or update an Agent](permissions/publish-agent.en-US.md)

## Reference Material Retention

Team owners and administrators open **Team settings → Reference Material Retention**. Choose a limit from 1 to 180 days and review the effect before saving. Shortening the limit also applies to previously saved reference material. Expired material will be cleared and tasks relying on it may stop.

A longer limit applies to newly saved material. Existing shorter expirations and chat transcripts follow their own rules. If a task loses a required reference, restore access or provide the material again in a new request.
