# Manage team members

Team owners and administrators can view members, invite or add colleagues, and manage which spaces they can enter in **Team settings → Team & People → People**. The page shows the member list by default. Search by nickname or email, filter by team role, and page through the results. Members and pending invites share the same **Rows per page** setting: 10 by default on a phone and 25 on a desktop, and you can change it to 10, 25, or 50. With several pages, use the pagination above or below the list. Search, filters, page, and page size are kept in the page link. After filtering, the count on the tab shows the number of matches. Switch to **Pending Invites** to find invitations by email and work through unfinished ones page by page; if nothing matches, change or clear the search. On a desktop you can expand the settings groups to find a section; on narrow screens use the **Settings** selector to switch sections.

## Invite or add colleagues

- Cloud edition: choose **Invite people** and enter an email address. When the window offers a choice of how the person joins, you can have them join specific spaces as soon as they accept, or join the team only and assign spaces later; without that choice, the person joins the team only and you assign spaces afterwards as described below. When you choose specific spaces, a space list opens: you can search it and select up to 20 spaces at a time, the list shows how many spaces match, and you can remove selected spaces at the top. All selected spaces use the same initial role. Before sending, check the email, how they join, the number of spaces, and the role at the bottom; if no space is selected, the window tells you what's missing. After sending, read the message: if the email wasn't sent, copy the invitation link and give it to the person through a suitable channel.
- Cloud batch invites: in the same window switch to **Batch Invite** and enter one email per line, or separate them with commas or semicolons. **Import one-column email CSV** fills the email list from a file for you to review. Each batch takes up to 25 distinct addresses; the bottom shows how many are valid and how many are duplicates. Addresses with format errors must be fixed before you can send. When the window offers a choice of how people join, everyone gets the same initial space access you chose. After submitting, a result is shown for each person; if someone's email wasn't sent, copy that person's invitation link from the results.
- Private deployment: choose **Add Member** and enter a name and email. The member joins the team immediately. On the completion page, copy the one-time link for setting a password and give it to the person through a secure channel; the link expires after 15 minutes. Then choose **Configure Space Access** to add the spaces the member needs.

If the email already belongs to a team member, the page says so and doesn't create a duplicate member.

## Manage space access

In the member row's **More** menu, choose **Manage Space Access**. Review the spaces already granted first; choose **Add Space** to search other spaces and grant a role. After changing the role for a granted space, choose **Save Role** for it to take effect; the page shows the result, and warns you about unsaved role changes before closing. When you're done, choose **Close** to return to the member list. Removing space access affects only that space and does not remove the member from the team. After removal, the person generally can no longer view or change conversations, reports, and plans in that space (including their own), and global search no longer shows them (files they uploaded to their personal knowledge base are not affected); they can still stop their own running conversations. Reports others shared with them stay readable according to the sharing settings. Granting access to the space again restores everything.

## Remove members

In the member row's **More** menu, choose **Remove from Team** and review the confirmation. In the cloud edition this removes the person from the current team and revokes their space access in this team; in a private deployment it also deactivates their account in that deployment and ends their current sessions. The owner, yourself, and some sole administrators can't be removed directly; the page tells you which handover is needed first.

To remove several members, select members on the current page, or use **Select removable members on this page** (on a phone, above the member cards), then choose **Remove Selected**, review the list and impact, and type the number of people to confirm. Selection is limited to the current page; after changing the search, team role, page, or refreshing the list, select again. When processing finishes, the numbers of successes and failures are shown separately, and failed members are not retried automatically. The owner and yourself can't be selected, and other required administrator protections are still checked for each person.

## View a member's credits

If you have permission to view credits, **View Credits** appears in the member row and opens that member's credit details with balance and usage. Credit transactions are shown page by page and can be filtered by type; reservations, refunds, additional charges, and released reservations from draft generation are labeled separately. New model charges show model names and expandable token categories and rates; see [View and allocate credits](credits.en-US.md). Accounts without this permission don't see the entry.
