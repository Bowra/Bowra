# Atlas Helpdesk — synthetic proposal

Invented vendor and commercial terms for this example only. Proposal ID: SYN-ATLAS-01. Currency: USD. Prices exclude tax. No real vendor is represented.

## 1. Scope and fees

- 80 named users at $18 per user per month, billed annually.
- One-time onboarding and migration: $4,000.
- Required support package: $2,400 per year.
- No usage charges within the stated scope. Additional users cost $18 per month, prorated for remaining whole months.

## 2. Delivery and term

- Planned launch: six weeks after receiving a complete source export and agreeing the migration mapping.
- Initial contract: 12 months. Renewal needs a new written agreement.
- Migration includes up to 20,000 tickets and one training session.

## 3. Data and service

- Role-based access and encryption in transit and at rest are stated in the proposal.
- The stated availability target is 99.9%; measurement details and service-credit terms have not been supplied.
- At exit, ticket records are exported as CSV and attachments as a ZIP archive without an additional fee. Export mapping is supplied.
- A security questionnaire and a sample exit export are available on request; neither is attached.

## 4. Assumptions

The customer supplies a clean source export. Data cleansing, additional training and custom integrations are outside scope and need a separate quote.
