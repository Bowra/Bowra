# Beacon Service Desk — synthetic proposal

Invented vendor and commercial terms for this example only. Proposal ID: SYN-BEACON-01. Currency: USD. Prices exclude tax. No real vendor is represented.

## 1. Scope and fees

- 80 named users at $15 per user per month, billed annually.
- One-time onboarding and migration: $2,500.
- Required support package: $3,600 per year.
- No usage charges within the stated scope. Additional users cost $15 per month, prorated for remaining whole months.

## 2. Delivery and term

- Planned launch: ten weeks after receiving a complete source export.
- Minimum non-cancellable term: 24 months. The second year's seat and support fees match the first year; onboarding is charged once.
- Migration includes up to 20,000 tickets and two training sessions.

## 3. Data and service

- Role-based access and encryption in transit and at rest are stated in the proposal.
- The stated availability target is 99.9%; measurement details and service-credit terms have not been supplied.
- At exit, ticket records are exported as CSV and attachments as a ZIP archive without an additional fee. Export mapping is supplied.
- A security questionnaire and a sample exit export are available on request; neither is attached.

## 4. Assumptions

No accelerated launch is included. A shorter term or earlier launch requires a revised proposal; neither is promised here.
