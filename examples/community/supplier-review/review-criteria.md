# Expected review criteria

All amounts are synthetic USD, excluding tax. These are comparison checks, not a claim that a procurement workflow ran.

## Arithmetic

| Supplier | First-year calculation       | First year | Minimum commitment at 80 users | Add 20 users in months 7–12 | Revised first year |
| -------- | ---------------------------- | ---------: | -----------------------------: | --------------------------: | -----------------: |
| Atlas    | 80 × 18 × 12 + 4,000 + 2,400 |    $23,680 |            $23,680 / 12 months |        20 × 18 × 6 = $2,160 |            $25,840 |
| Beacon   | 80 × 15 × 12 + 2,500 + 3,600 |    $20,500 |            $38,500 / 24 months |        20 × 15 × 6 = $1,800 |            $22,300 |
| Cedar    | 22,000 + 1,500               |    $23,500 |            $23,500 / 12 months |        20 × 22 × 6 = $2,640 |            $26,140 |

Beacon's minimum commitment is `2 × (80 × 15 × 12 + 3,600) + 2,500 = 38,500`. Do not count its first year as the whole non-cancellable obligation. Growth sensitivity covers the first year only; it does not assume a second-year seat count.

## Evidence and decision quality

- Atlas meets the stated baseline budget and launch timing. Its data export scope is the most complete among proposals that meet the deadline. A reasonable recommendation remains conditional on security evidence, export testing and the migration prerequisites.
- Beacon is cheapest in year one, but its ten-week delivery misses the deadline and its two-year term conflicts with the preference. An earlier launch or shorter contract needs a revised quote.
- Cedar is fastest and fits the initial budget. Encryption at rest, attachment export and exit charges are unspecified. The answer should ask for evidence, not turn missing information into a categorical failure or a guarantee.
- With the specified growth, Atlas exceeds the budget by $1,840 and Cedar by $2,140. Beacon remains within the first-year budget, but its original timing and term issues remain.
- No proposal supplies audited security evidence or complete availability remedies. Quoted targets are assertions from the inputs.
- A good memo names uncertainty, cites sections and explains what would change the recommendation. A weighted score is optional; any weights must be disclosed rather than presented as facts.
