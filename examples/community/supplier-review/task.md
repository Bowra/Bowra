# Copyable task

Review the three attached synthetic helpdesk proposals for 80 users. Our first-year budget is USD 24,000 before tax, our launch deadline is eight weeks, and we prefer a 12-month commitment. At exit we need both ticket data and attachments in usable formats.

Write a concise decision memo with:

1. A comparison table showing recurring fees, one-time fees, first-year total, minimum committed contract cost, launch timing and exit coverage. Show the arithmetic.
2. Clear distinctions between a stated fact, a missing term and your judgment. Cite the proposal filename and section for each decisive claim.
3. A conditional recommendation, the reasons alternatives are weaker, and three follow-up questions that could change the recommendation. Do not invent security certifications or verify vendors on the web.
4. A sensitivity table for 20 additional users starting in month 7; support and onboarding fees stay unchanged. Flag any first-year budget overruns.

Use only these proposals. Label the scenario synthetic. Do not send a vendor message, sign a contract, or claim that a Word file exists unless a file was actually created.
